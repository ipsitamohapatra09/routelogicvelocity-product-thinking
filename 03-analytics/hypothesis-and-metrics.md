## Finalized product hypothesis

> Based on UXR-04 and the workflow and satisfaction data (compliance checks take 14.6 minutes against a 3-minute benchmark, with coordinator CSAT of 2.2/5), I believe that streamlining compliance logging and shift handoff for frontline coordinators will help them complete the dispatch workflow in RouteLogic Velocity instead of relying on manual workarounds. I expect this to reduce median daily compliance-check time by 50%, from 14.6 minutes to about 7 minutes. I will protect manager Compliance CSAT (at least 3.9) and compliance-record completeness, and make a go/no-go decision after a six-week pilot across 3–5 accounts, following a two-week baseline.

## Success metrics

| Metric | Type | Target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Share of coordinators completing the dispatch workflow through shift handoff in the product | North-star | Increase from 31% toward 50% during the pilot | Captures the intended behavior change: keeping coordinators in the product through handoff instead of finishing through texts, WhatsApp, or paper. |
| Median time to log compliance checks per coordinator per day | Leading indicator | Reduce by 50%, from 14.6 minutes to about 7 minutes, by week 6 | Measures the largest identified workflow time gap and should respond sooner than retention, NPS, or churn. |
| Manager Compliance CSAT and compliance-record completeness | Guardrail | CSAT remains at or above 3.9; record completeness does not decline | Ensures faster frontline logging does not reduce the quality of records or shift the burden to managers and renewal buyers. |

**Pilot decision rule:** Scale if compliance-check time falls by at least 50% and guardrails hold; iterate at a 25–49% reduction; pivot or stop below 25%. Pause if manager Compliance CSAT falls below 3.5 or records become less complete.
